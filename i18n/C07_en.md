# This was an exercise

This message was part of a Security team awareness exercise. No data was sent, and nobody is being judged: these emails are designed to be convincing.

**Theme:** Mailbox security alert

## The clues you could have spotted

- **Errors**: spelling mistake in the subject line, grammar mistake in the body
- **Technical indicators**: sender domain from outside the company, link to an unknown domain, reply-to address differs from the sender
- **Visual presentation**: low-quality logo, layout differs from the genuine email
- **Language and content**: generic greeting ('Dear user'), unusual request
- **Manipulation tactics**: urgency, threat of consequences, promise of a reward

## The right reflex

1. When in doubt, click **Report**.
2. If you entered a password, tell the Security team: no blame, we just act faster.
3. Check any unusual request through another channel (phone, intranet).
